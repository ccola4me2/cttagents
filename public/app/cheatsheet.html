Cheat sheet

The rules that cost money when they are got wrong, and that clicking around will never tell you.

The money rule

The client pays the supplier, always. This portal records what is owed and chases it. It never takes a payment and never holds one.

Money always shows cents: $289.00, never $289.

The ninety day trap

The one that costs a client their cabin.

Inside ninety days of departure a cruise is usually paid in full at booking. The whole trip cost sits in the deposit field.

So a balance is never gross minus deposit. In this case that comes to nothing, no row is written, and the largest reservations in the book end up with an empty schedule that nothing can chase.

Reminders come from the schedule

Not from the final payment date. A date on its own warns nobody: every reminder is built from the payment schedule, so a reservation with no lines has nothing to chase.

Fill in the gaps lists every reservation in that state with the amount sitting on no line. Press Build schedule.

Why it says past due when you paid it

There are two lines for the same money. The reminder sits ten days in front of the vendor deadline.

Posting against the reminder is moved to the deadline for you, but a reminder left over from an older schedule can still look like a balance. Opening the reservation shows both.

How your share is decided

Three answers, in this order:

  • a percentage written on that reservation
  • then your standing agreement
  • then all of it

Your agreement can hold two rates: one for business you generate, a lower one for leads the agency hands you. A reservation marked company lead uses the second.

Where a figure has a smaller number beside it, the smaller one is your share. The larger is the commission on the booking.

Nothing agreed is not nought

No split recorded means you keep what you earned. A deliberate 0 means the agency keeps everything, which is a real arrangement for a house account. They are different facts and the portal treats them differently.

Yours whatever the split says

Tour conductor credit. Vendor bonus.

Those are earned by filling a group or hitting a target, not by the agency's terms with the vendor, so you keep the lot.

Casino rates are the exception and are split like anything else, because those run on the agency's licence and its risk.

Your own travel is never split and the agency takes none of it. It is also left out of production totals, because it is not client business.

Statuses

Reservation: quoted, booked, travelled, cancelled.
Cancelled is excluded from every total.

Commission: pending, received, none.
None is not nought and not pending: it is a trip that never earns, such as a courtesy booking. Marking it stops it sitting in the chase list forever waiting for money nobody is going to send.

Who can see what

You see your own commissions. You never see another advisor's. Whoever runs the agency sees everything.

The same goes for your clients, leads and the trips under a supplier: yours only. The supplier directory itself, contacts and rates, is shared.

Clients never see commission anywhere, on any page, ever.

Pricing lines

One line per thing. Fare, taxes and port fees, gratuities, drinks, extras are all commissioned differently, so a single lump sum is the commonest way a commission comes out wrong.

Two travellers means two fare lines. The client sees them added up, not listed twice.

A family in two cabins

Quote it as one reservation, book it as two.

While they are deciding they want one page with one total, so put both cabins on a single reservation as named pricing lines: "Balcony 8102, Mum and Dad" and "Interior 6210, the boys".

Once they say yes the cruise line gives you two confirmation numbers, so split it.

One holiday, several reservations

Tick them on Reservations and press Put together as one trip. The client gets one link with everything in date order.

Only booked or travelled reservations go in. A quote never does. A reservation is in one trip at a time, and a cancelled one drops off by itself.

Itinerary from a confirmation

Read a confirmation on a reservation's itinerary proposes lines from a PDF. Check every time: nothing is saved until you add it, and a wrong time goes in front of the client.

A time with no am or pm is read as 24 hour. A scan is a picture and cannot be read. The client sees it only when Client sees it is ticked.

Group proposals and contracts

Upload the PDF on a new group and check what it filled in. A cruise category is the room type. If the cabins in a category do not add up to the quote's own totals, the rate grid is left empty and the fares go in the notes as printed.

Nothing saves until you press the button at the bottom.

How the client's page groups trips

Reservations whose dates overlap, give or take two days, are drawn as one holiday with one total. Not "the same fortnight".

Quotes sit in their own block, one row each, because a quote is a question and two questions are not one holiday.

When the numbers do not add up

Commission total looks wrong? It is almost always one of three:

  • a no commission reservation, excluded on purpose
  • a cancelled one, excluded from every total
  • you are comparing the commission on the booking with your share

Cannot find a client you know is there

The Clients page defaults to people who have booked. Search finds prospects too and says so, or switch the filter at the top to Everyone.

Nothing is being emailed

Almost always the agency's mail setup, not anything you did, and not something you can see or fix from your side. Tell whoever runs the agency.

Nothing is lost while it is sorted: a send with no mail key waits rather than marking everybody failed, so it goes out once the setup is right.

Where to look it up

How this works is the full manual.
