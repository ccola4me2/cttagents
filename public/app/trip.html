How the page is built

The day by day comes from the dates on each reservation: a hotel shows on the day it starts and the day it ends, a flight on each way. For more, such as flight times, a dinner or a tour, add lines to a reservation's itinerary and mark it ready to share; they appear on the right day.

Payments, documents and messages stay on each reservation's own page, which your client opens from this one.
