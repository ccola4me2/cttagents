Paste from a spreadsheet

Copy the rows out of a spreadsheet and paste them here, one cabin to a line. If the first line has headings (Cabin, Category, Guests, Guest names, Booked by, Booking number, Status, TC, Notes) they are read for what each column is. Without headings the columns are taken in that order. They are added to what is already on the sheet.
