Forward a confirmation by email

Forward the vendor's confirmation email, or one with its PDF attached, to your own address below. It waits here until you open it and check what was read. Nothing is made into a reservation until you press the button.

Paste one confirmation

Paste the confirmation email from the vendor and this reads what it can off it: the number, the ship, the dates, the cabin, the guests, the money. Nothing is saved until you have checked it, and every field shows the line it came from, because vendor confirmations have no standard shape and this will misread some of them.

Drop a PDF, a screenshot or photo, or a saved email here or choose files. You can also paste a screenshot with Ctrl or Cmd and V. Up to 5 files, read together as one reservation.

A PDF is read in your browser: only the text it contains is sent to be read. A saved email is opened by the portal and not kept. A screenshot or photo is read by Cloudflare's AI inside this portal's own account and not kept either. The file itself goes onto the reservation only if you tick Keep this PDF below. Then check what it found, the same as pasting.
