The front

Shows as "Prepared for Sarah and Mike". Leave it empty to say just "A proposal".

JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 5 MB. A wide picture works best.

What is in it

One section for each thing: the cruise, the hotel, a day, an excursion. Each can have a picture, some words and a price. Anything you leave empty is left off.

Price and link

Prices are whatever you type, so include the cents yourself. The page says prices and availability are subject to change.

Send it by email

The email carries your words and a button that opens the proposal. It answers to you. Nothing is saved to a client record.
