Promotions and extras

Onboard credit, a free drinks package, a reduced deposit, anything that goes with this offer. They show on the proposal under "Included with this offer" and are not added to the price. When the quote is accepted each one goes onto the reservation as an amenity to chase. Who is paying for it is for you and is not shown to your client.

What is in it

One section for each thing: the cruise, the hotel, a day, an excursion. Each can have a picture, some words and a price. Anything you leave empty is left off.

Prices are whatever you type, so include the cents yourself. The page says prices and availability are subject to change.
