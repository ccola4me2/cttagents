How this works

  • Start a group under Groups. Upload the cruise line's group proposal or contract and the details fill in for you to check, then set up the group's page and its sign-up form.
  • Each group has an owner, the person who started it. The owner's name, logo and phone and email are on the group's page and receive its sign-ups, and the owner earns the tour conductor credits. Set how your own pages read under My pages.
  • Open a group's cabin sheet from this list to see every cabin, who booked it and their own booking number. Anybody on the desk can work on any group's sheet.
  • Credits are one for every twelve guests across everybody's bookings, and cancelled cabins do not count.
  • The owner's own CTT bookings for Margaritaville at Sea come onto a group's sheet by themselves when their group number is the group's number, and stay up to date as the reservation changes. One that sails the same day but has no group number is offered above the sheet. They show the cabin, category, guest names, booking number and whether it is paid, and never any money.
