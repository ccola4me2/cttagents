Client emails

The notes a client gets on their own after a reservation is booked, in the advisor's name, with nothing for the advisor to do.

When this is on, every reservation made from then on gets the emails below, planned the moment it is booked: a note that the trip is booked, reminders before the deposit and the final payment, a nudge about travel documents, online check-in, bon voyage, and a welcome home. Reservations that already exist are never written to. An advisor can skip any one email, or all of them, on the reservation. A client who unsubscribes is never sent another.

What each one says
